Living Heritage

Culture & Tradition

Kerala's artistic soul shines through its classical dance forms, ritual performances, and vibrant temple traditions.

Kathakali

Kathakali

A classical dance-drama known for its elaborate costumes, vivid face paint, and powerful gestures — telling epic stories through movement.

Mohiniyattam

Mohiniyattam

The "dance of the enchantress" — graceful, fluid, and deeply expressive, performed in pristine white and gold attire.

Theyyam

Theyyam

A sacred ritual art form from northern Kerala where performers embody deities in spectacular costumes and intense trance-like devotion.

Temple Festivals

Temple Festivals

Grand processions with caparisoned elephants, rhythmic percussion ensembles, and thousands of lamps illuminating the night.

A Taste of Kerala

Culinary Treasures

Kerala's cuisine is a symphony of spices, coconut, and fresh ingredients — each dish a story of the land.

Kerala Biryani

Fragrant rice layered with spiced meat, caramelized onions, and aromatic saffron — a Malabar specialty.

Appam & Stew

Lacy rice pancakes with a creamy coconut milk stew — the perfect harmony of delicate and rich.

Puttu & Kadala

Steamed cylinders of rice flour layered with coconut, served with spiced black chickpeas — a beloved breakfast.

Ada Payasam

A luxurious dessert of rice parcels slow-cooked in coconut milk and jaggery — pure indulgence in every spoonful.

Celebrations of Life

Vibrant Festivals

Kerala's festivals burst with color, rhythm, and joy — bringing communities together in spectacular celebration.

Onam

The grand harvest festival with flower carpets, traditional feasts, and the iconic snake boat races.

Vishu

The Malayali New Year, marked by the auspicious Vishukkani — a ritual arrangement of golden flowers and fruits.

Thrissur Pooram

The "mother of all festivals" — a breathtaking spectacle of elephants, drums, and fireworks.
